# Contributing

Thanks for taking an interest in Medisync.

## Before proposing a change

- Keep demo records fictional; never add patient, prescription, contact, or facility data from a real person or organization.
- Keep risk calculations explainable and document their assumptions.
- Do not imply that traceability anomalies prove a product is falsified.
- Do not add real ordering, prescription changes, or caregiver messaging without the required professional review, consent, and integrations.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, readable contrast, text resizing, and reduced-motion support.

## Local preview

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The static site has no build step or package dependencies.

## Changes

Describe the user problem, what changed, and how you reviewed the interaction. For changes to risk scoring, update `docs/risk-model.md` and the visible explanation in the site together.
