# Three-minute demo guide

## 0:00–0:25 — The problem

“Medicine stockouts and delayed shipments can leave teams reacting late. Medisync brings stock, consumption, expiry, supplier, and shipment signals together so a warning includes both its reason and a next step.”

## 0:25–1:10 — Risk Radar

Open the interactive Risk Radar and select **Amoxicillin**. Point out the five-day stock estimate, two-day shipment delay, risk score, and weighted factor breakdown. Explain that the numbers are simulated and the scoring method is deliberately visible.

## 1:10–1:50 — Supplier decision support

Open **Find a backup supplier**. Keep the initial need at 1,000 units, two days, 25 km, and at least 85% reliability. Show why Supplier A qualifies and why the other examples fail. Emphasize that the site compares options but does not place an order.

## 1:50–2:20 — Batch trace

Open the batch timeline. Follow the sample lot from manufacturer to distributor to hospital pharmacy. Point out that each event is labeled **Recorded via scan · Demo** and explain that an anomaly would ask for review rather than claim counterfeiting.

## 2:20–2:45 — Refill preview and accessibility

Show the optional refill-plan preview and its confirmation step. Demonstrate text enlargement or high contrast. State that caregiver updates require consent and that no messages or orders are sent by this prototype.

## 2:45–3:00 — Close

“Medisync turns a supply signal into an explainable decision workflow. The next step is connecting validated inventory, supplier, and shipment services with authorized review.”

## Before presenting

- Open the site and check all demo controls in the presentation browser.
- Use the rubric from the specific event or course; criteria and weights vary.
- Say plainly which records and integrations are simulated.
- Be ready to explain the score weights and the limits of the prototype.
