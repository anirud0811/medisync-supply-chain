How is the risk score calculated?

Each factor is scored from 0–100, multiplied by its published weight, and combined into one score. Low is 0–29, Moderate 30–54, High 55–74, Critical 75–100. This is a transparent demo model, not a validated clinical or operational forecasting system.

Transparent by design

Useful signals, honest limits.

Medisync is a decision-support prototype. It makes assumptions visible and leaves high-impact actions with authorized people.

Risk model used in this demo

Each input factor is scored 0–100; weighted contributions combine into an overall score.

Stockout proximity30%
Shipment delay20%
Supplier reliability15%
Expiry exposure15%
Unusual inventory movement20%

What this demo does not claim

  • Inventory, suppliers, and scan events are fictional demo records.
  • No live GPS, supplier connection, barcode hardware, or orders are connected.
  • A movement anomaly flags a record for review; it does not prove counterfeiting.
  • Refill suggestions do not change prescriptions or replace professional approval.
  • SMS, WhatsApp, authentication, and patient data storage are not connected.
